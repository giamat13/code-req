Privacy

code-req stores only what it needs to deliver requests between people and developers. It has no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party trackers.

What is stored

A request keeps the title, description, platforms, optional icon, notes, budget, deadline, and the requester's name. Contact details (email, phone, availability) are stored separately and are readable only by the developer. A developer profile keeps the username, display name, bio, and accepted platforms.

Who can see it

Developers see the requests sent to them. Anyone with a tracking link can see that one request. Developer profiles and portfolios are public. The site's operator can see only what the database rules allow.

Accounts

Requesters do not need an account. Submitting a request creates an anonymous session in your browser, so you can find your requests again on this device. Developers sign in with Google or with an email address and password. Anonymous sessions cannot manage requests.

Your rights

Developers can export all their data as JSON and delete their account from the dashboard. Requesters can cancel a request while it is new, and they can ask a developer to remove a request.

Browser storage

The site stores a few preferences in your browser: language, theme, and the drafts you are writing. It does not set tracking cookies.
